## Work sequence and exit gates

The BCH launch bundle verifier (`v4/blaze/src/graduation/launch-bundle.ts`) checks the creator position's unique non-minting genesis, reconstructs the exact curve and fee market from the manifest and graduation terms, and hashes those identities with the position genesis txid and creator holder script. Token genesis publishes the manifest hash in `MBLZ` and the bundle hash separately in `MBND`; independent verifiers require each exact commitment. The [E1 chipnet run](08-chipnet-e1-evidence.md) confirmed both genesis transactions and commitments. Reorg handling and bundle format freeze remain release work.

The BCH curve buy, sell and claim planners have a shared signed builder for the V1/V2 trading ABI. A reachable local V2 lifecycle signs an opening buy, sell, terminal buy, three curve claims and graduation, then spends the locked bin and claims all three market fee balances. Its 20 graduation-fixture checks pass. The 90% graduation transaction is 3,302 bytes at a 4,000-sat fee. The [E1 chipnet run](08-chipnet-e1-evidence.md) then confirmed this lifecycle from real genesis UTXOs; broader relay and worst-case policy review remain open.

Creator position NFT genesis has a separate offline seed/genesis builder and verifier (`v4/blaze/src/graduation/position-genesis.ts`). Nine local assertions pass, including a BCH VM spend. It enforces a single non-minting NFT, zero fungible amount, and delivery to the creator's script. Before release, the manifest must commit the verified category and genesis transaction evidence; the graduation covenant currently pins the owned-bin script but does not prove the NFT's provenance. The BCH V2 graduation prototype now enforces a fixed 10% per-side band around the terminal marginal curve price in both lock paths. The constructor bounds every price-comparison product to signed VM range; 16 graduation VM checks and 20 constructor assertions pass. This is a prototype parameter change and requires a fresh curve script hash in the draft manifest.

These phases are capability groups. The token-quote design must inform the common manifest and graduation interface while the BCH curve is built. Each gate needs passing VM tests and a transaction builder that independently checks covenant outputs.

1. **Genesis and launch manifest:** freeze the binary manifest and state commitment; enforce carrier, dust, fee-rate, allocation, NFT capability and hosted BCMR checks in the offline builder. Add a read-only chipnet verifier, then create one fresh chipnet fixed-supply FT and record txids. The current 15 genesis, 14 manifest and 4 metadata checks are local evidence only.
2. **BCH curve safety:** creator, partner and protocol whole-balance claims and terminal partial-fill/refund layout are compiled and VM tested. The constructor derives fee-recipient hashes from the manifest, and unsigned buy/sell/claim plans exist. Wire wallet signing and broadcast; test hostile output ordering, categories, reserve and fee arithmetic, and completed-state rejection. Measure worst-case VM cost and policy relay acceptance.
3. **CashToken quote curve:** implement paired base and quote state UTXOs with one atomic spend, exact category conservation, independent BCH carriers and common fee accounting. Add builders and VM tests for missing or substituted vault inputs. Complete a chipnet token-quote trade cycle.
4. **Completion and market handoff:** use a permissionless terminal state for each quote type. Prove exact remaining fill, explicit refund, fee extraction, destination funding and price/depth bounds. Test both graduation paths and execute both on chipnet.
5. **Permanent liquidity and fees:** implement fee-bearing markets and an irrevocable lock for at least 90% of graduation liquidity. Keep a separately disclosed creator-owned share of at most 10%. Continue creator, partner and protocol fee rights after graduation. Test withdrawals, claims, direct token/token swaps and locked-principal attacks.
6. **Presale and Alpha:** implement Presale success, failure and refunds with its destination fixed before deposits. Implement fixed Alpha allocations and a bounded pre-public purchase mode; settle before public trading and disclose quantities. Exercise claims, expiry and adversarial receipts on chipnet.
7. **Products and release:** provide an SDK, launch creation and trading UI, indexer/reorg handling, artifact registry, operator runbook and external review. Release after the complete lifecycle is independently reproducible from txids and artifact hashes.

## Next implementation slice

The next implementation slice is CashToken-quoted E2: signed paired-vault transactions, terminal refund, token/token graduation and a fee-bearing market lifecycle. The paired token-quote buy, sell and claim paths already spend both covenants atomically in the BCH VM with manifest-derived unsigned plans. Follow E2 with Presale and both Alpha modes, then SDK/indexer/UI integration. Both quote paths still require worst-case cost and relay-policy review.

## Commit discipline

One meaningful contract/builder boundary per commit, with source, vectors, tests and short evidence in the same diff. Avoid mixing V4 frozen contract edits with Blaze work. A commit is ready only after its local gate passes; chipnet txids are added in a follow-up evidence commit.
